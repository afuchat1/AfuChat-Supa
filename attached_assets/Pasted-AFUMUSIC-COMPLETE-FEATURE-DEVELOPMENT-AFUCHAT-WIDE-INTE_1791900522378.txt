PART 5 — FREE AND PAID MUSIC PUBLISHING

5.1 Two publishing options

The publishing interface must clearly offer:

Publish as Free

- Available to eligible creators without requiring AfuChat Premium.
- Price is zero.
- Listeners can listen without purchasing the track.
- Track is labeled Free.

Publish as Paid

- Available only to creators with a valid, active AfuChat Premium entitlement.
- Requires a valid price between 5 and 1,000 ACoin inclusive.
- Track is labeled Paid or Premium throughout AfuChat.
- Listeners must purchase the track before accessing protected playback.

Do not combine Free and Paid into an ambiguous price input that could accidentally publish the wrong access type.

5.2 Premium publishing enforcement

When a non-Premium creator selects Paid publishing, show this exact message:

"Paid music publishing requires AfuChat Premium. Upgrade your subscription to publish music for sale."

Provide an Upgrade to Premium action that opens the existing AfuChat subscription screen.

Preserve the selected file, metadata, and cover when practical so the creator can continue after upgrading.

Verify Premium entitlement through the trusted existing subscription system. A client-side flag, locally stored value, or hidden UI control is not sufficient.

If a Premium subscription expires or is cancelled, enforce the existing policy for new publishing and management without unexpectedly deleting previously published tracks or changing their prices.

5.3 ACoin price rules

Every newly published Paid track must have an integer price between 5 and 1,000 ACoin inclusive.

- Minimum: 5 ACoin.
- Maximum: 1,000 ACoin.
- No paid track at zero ACoin.
- No price below 5 ACoin.
- No price above 1,000 ACoin.
- No negative, malformed, missing, or invalid paid price.

Validate the range in the publishing UI and at a trusted backend/database boundary.

A modified client must not be able to bypass the restriction.

Free publishing remains a separate option with a zero price.

Do not confuse the ACoin price of an individual track with the AfuChat Premium subscription price.
