---
name: Wallet payment and withdrawal policy
description: The chosen top-up options and current withdrawal eligibility messaging.
---

Keep all three top-up options: Play/App Store billing when store products are available, plus Whop and Pesapal. If store products are unavailable, users should still be able to choose either hosted provider.

Withdrawals are not available yet. The wallet should show a coming-soon action, explain that the user is not eligible yet, and encourage continued app use rather than initiating a payout.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose all three payment methods and said withdrawals are a future feature with access not yet open.

**How to apply:** Keep Whop and Pesapal visible on mobile even when store billing has no products; expose store billing only when its products load. Do not add or imply a live withdrawal flow until the user changes this policy.
