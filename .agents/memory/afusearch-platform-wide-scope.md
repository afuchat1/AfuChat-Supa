---
name: AfuSearch platform-wide scope
description: Product direction for AfuSearch coverage across platform content.
---

AfuSearch is intended to act as a platform-wide search engine that indexes searchable content across AfuChat, rather than being limited to image matching or search suggestions.

**Why:** the user explicitly set this as the product direction.

**How to apply:** when expanding AfuSearch, include the platform's relevant content categories and clarify authorization rules for private content before indexing it.
