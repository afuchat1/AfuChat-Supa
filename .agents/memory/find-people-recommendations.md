---
name: Find People recommendations
description: AfuChat rules for ranking new people and keeping the Find People layout unchanged.
---

Prioritize people the user has not recently seen whenever fresh candidates exist. Rank candidates by shared interests and mutual connections. Do not add layout elements; remove the “Suggested for you” label and its icon.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested this behavior for AfuChat's Find People page.

**How to apply:** When changing Find People recommendations, preserve the existing layout and rotate through eligible profiles before repeating recently shown people.
