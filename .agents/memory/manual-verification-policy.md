---
name: AfuChat manual verification policy
description: User-stated values for personal verification eligibility and applicant guidance.
---

Personal account verification should be earned through sustained, authentic participation and public relevance. It should not be open to every account or depend on vanity metrics. Keep explanations focused on the values, and show unqualified users what to do before checking again.

**Why:** The user wants a meaningful qualification process that rewards continued use of AfuChat without overexplaining the product.

**How to apply:** Keep the application gate enforced server-side. Present the requirements and next steps briefly, and let users recheck eligibility after meeting them. Treat exact numeric thresholds as product settings that can be revised separately.
